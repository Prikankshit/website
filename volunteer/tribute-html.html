The Problem

Fragmented Coordination Slows Everything Down

Organizations struggle with disjointed tools, manual processes, and zero visibility into their volunteer operations.

📋

Manual Assignment

Assigning volunteers to shifts by spreadsheet is error-prone and doesn't consider skills or availability.

📡

No Real-Time Visibility

Supervisors have no live view of who's checked in, who's late, or what roles remain unfilled during an event.

🗂️

Siloed Systems

Scheduling, communications, and reporting live in separate tools with no unified source of truth.

📞

Chaotic Communication

Last-minute changes require frantic calls and texts with no formal escalation path.

✦ The Solution

One Unified Platform for Everything

Tribute brings together scheduling, attendance, AI matching, and supervisor tools under a single, intuitive experience.

Centralized Scheduling

Browse, filter, and sign up for shifts with calendar integration and real-time seat availability.

Live Attendance Tracking

QR-code check-in and supervisor dashboards give instant visibility across all active shifts.

AI-Powered Auto-Match

Automatically assign the right volunteers to the right roles based on certified skills and availability.

Role-Based Access

Tailored views for Admins, Supervisors, and Volunteers — everyone sees exactly what they need.

Feature 01

Volunteer Dashboard

A personalized home base where volunteers see their upcoming shifts, hours logged, and quick actions — all at a glance.

  • Upcoming shift reminders & countdowns
  • Cumulative hours tracking
  • One-tap check-in access
  • Notification center for new opportunities
Feature 02

Shift Planning & Discovery

Volunteers browse available shifts by date, category, location, and required role — and sign up in seconds.

  • Interactive calendar with event dots
  • Filter by skill, role & location
  • Add to calendar (iCal & Google)
  • Real-time seat availability
Feature 03

Active Shift Tracking

During a live event, volunteers and supervisors have real-time tools to manage attendance, log incidents, and track assets.

  • Live check-in / check-out
  • Incident reporting workflow
  • Asset check-out management
  • Shift close-out & summary
Feature 04

AI Auto-Match

Admins trigger smart auto-assignment that scores every volunteer against required shift skills, maximizing coverage in one click.

  • Skill-based scoring algorithm
  • Respects volunteer hour caps
  • Manual override supported
  • Waitlist management
Feature 05

Platform Administration

A full admin suite for creating events, managing rosters, sending communications, and viewing analytics across all activity.

  • Event creation with skill tags
  • Volunteer roster management
  • Multi-channel communications
  • Platform-wide analytics
